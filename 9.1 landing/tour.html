Beautiful Places of Bangladesh

Explore some of the most naturally beautiful destinations of our country.

01. Cox's Bazar

Location: Chattogram

How to go: Bus, Train or Flight from Dhaka

Cost: ৳4,700 - ৳8,100

Visitors/year: Very high, especially holidays

Stay: Hotels, Resorts & Guest Houses

02. Sajek Valley

Location: Rangamati

How to go: Dhaka → Khagrachhari → Sajek

Cost: ৳6,000 - ৳10,000

Visitors/year: Very popular in winter

Stay: Resorts, Cottages & Hotels

03. Bandarban

Location: Chattogram Division

How to go: Direct Bus from Dhaka

Cost: ৳7,000 - ৳12,000

Visitors/year: About 6.5-7 lakh

Stay: Hotels, Resorts & Cottages

04. Sundarbans

Location: Khulna & Mongla

How to go: Dhaka → Khulna → Mongla → Boat

Cost: Depends on tour package

Visitors/year: Mainly during winter

Stay: Hotels & Tour Boats
