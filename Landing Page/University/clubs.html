Student Societies Overview

Metropolitan University hosts more than 30 chartered student clubs overseen by the Office of Student Affairs. Each club provides practical experiential leadership, funded participation in international competitions, mentorship from prominent faculty advisors, and networking with industry alumni.

Metropolitan Programming Club (MPC)

Established in 1998, MPC is one of the nation's most active collegiate competitive programming and software development societies.

  • Core Activities: ICPC programming bootcamps, weekly algorithm mock contests on Codeforces/LeetCode, and open-source GitHub project sprints.
  • Flagship Event: "MetroHacks" – an annual 48-hour national hackathon with $15,000 in prizes and direct recruiting from Fortune 500 tech companies.
  • Meeting Schedule: Every Wednesday at 5:00 PM in Alan Turing Computer Lab 3.
  • Faculty Advisor: Dr. Alan Vance, Associate Professor of Computer Science.

Metropolitan Robotics Club (MRC)

MRC brings together electrical, mechanical, and software engineers to prototype autonomous vehicles, aerial drones, and bio-inspired robotics.

  • Core Activities: Autonomous navigation (ROS2), PCB micro-soldering workshops, 3D additive manufacturing, and computer vision integration.
  • Flagship Event: National University Rover Challenge (URC) & Annual BattleBots Arena.
  • Meeting Schedule: Every Tuesday & Thursday at 4:30 PM in the Mechatronics Fabrication Lab.
  • Faculty Advisor: Prof. Sarah Jensen, Department of Electrical Engineering.

Metropolitan Science Club (MSC)

Dedicated to popularizing scientific curiosity, astrophysics, applied biotechnology, and environmental sustainability among undergraduates.

  • Core Activities: Telescopic astronomy observation nights, science journalism publications, laboratory demonstration exhibits, and scientific paper reading circles.
  • Flagship Event: National Collegiate Science Olympiad and Youth Climate Innovation Summit.
  • Meeting Schedule: Every Monday at 4:00 PM in the Science Quad Seminar Room 102.
  • Faculty Advisor: Dr. Marcus Thorne, Professor of Physics & Materials Science.

Metropolitan Business Club (MBC)

The premier incubator for aspiring corporate leaders, venture capitalists, marketing strategists, and ethical startup founders.

  • Core Activities: Harvard case-competition simulations, equity portfolio pitch decks, corporate boardroom visits, and CEO fireside chats.
  • Flagship Event: "VentureQuest" – Annual Angel Pitch Competition awarding seed capital to viable student-founded ventures.
  • Meeting Schedule: Every Thursday at 5:30 PM in the Executive MBA Boardroom.
  • Faculty Advisor: Prof. Eleanor Brooks, Chair of Entrepreneurial Studies.

Club Membership & Registration Instructions

Any active university undergraduate or graduate student may register for up to three chartered clubs:

  1. Browse Club Catalog: Review the active club directory and identify societies aligned with your career or personal passions.
  2. Club Fair Enrollment: Sign up during the bi-annual Club Fair held in the Central Quad during the second week of each semester.
  3. Membership Profile: Complete the digital registration form with your Student ID number. No membership dues are required; all club activities are fully subsidized by the student activity fund.
  4. Attend General Body Meeting (GBM): Join the semester kick-off orientation meeting to meet executive committee leads and pick project teams.
