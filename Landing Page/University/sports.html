Athletics Department Overview

Metropolitan University is home to the "Metropolitan Blue Knights" varsity teams, competing across nine major collegiate disciplines. Our athletics program emphasizes academic integrity, rigorous conditioning, leadership training, and holistic student well-being.

All enrolled students enjoy complimentary access to Olympic-sized swimming facilities, strength conditioning centers, indoor arenas, and intramural tournament leagues throughout the academic calendar.

Varsity Football Program

The Metropolitan Football team competes at the national collegiate division. Led by licensed UEFA coaches, training focuses on tactical positioning, aerobic endurance, and set-piece execution.

  • Facilities: FIFA-certified 10,000-seat stadium with natural Bermuda grass turf, night-match floodlights, and player hydrotherapy recovery rooms.
  • Training Schedule: Monday – Thursday, 4:00 PM – 6:30 PM. Tactical film sessions on Friday mornings.
  • Competitions: National Inter-University Football Championship, Chancellor's Gold Cup, and annual Alumni Invitational.

Varsity Cricket Program

With a storied legacy of producing national league players, our cricket academy offers specialized net bowling machines, video biomechanical analysis, and tournament play in both T20 and 50-over formats.

  • Facilities: The University Oval featuring four first-class clay pitches, eight covered turf practice nets, and a digital pavilion scoreboard.
  • Training Schedule: Tuesday – Friday, 3:30 PM – 6:00 PM; Saturday inter-squad friendly matches.
  • Competitions: All-University Premier League (UPL) and National Collegiate Trophy.

How to Join & Registration Instructions

Whether aspiring to compete on a varsity roster or seeking recreational intramural leagues:

  1. Step 1: Physical Clearance: Obtain a sports physical clearance certificate from the University Health Center.
  2. Step 2: Attend Seasonal Tryouts: Open varsity walk-on tryouts take place during the first three weeks of each semester.
  3. Step 3: Registration Submission: Complete the digital athletics registration profile using your Student ID.
  4. Step 4: Orientation & Gear Issue: Selected team members attend the code-of-conduct briefing and receive official team jerseys and equipment.
