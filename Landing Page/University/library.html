Library Overview & Mission

The Metropolitan Central Library serves as the intellectual heart of the university campus. Spanning five modern floors, it provides an inspiring academic sanctuary for undergraduate students, graduate scholars, and faculty researchers.

With quiet reflection lounges, state-of-the-art multimedia presentation pods, assistive learning technologies, and a dedicated research consultation desk, our mission is to foster discovery, intellectual inquiry, and lifelong scholarship.

Digital Library, E-Books & Research Papers

Members enjoy unrestricted 24/7 off-campus and on-campus access to leading global scholarly repositories:

  • Online Peer-Reviewed Journals: Direct access to IEEE Xplore, ScienceDirect, JSTOR, SpringerLink, Nature Portfolio, and ACM Digital Library.
  • Digital E-Books Collection: Over 1,200,000 downloadable academic e-books via ProQuest, Wiley Online Library, and Oxford Academic.
  • Research Paper Repository: Institutional digital repository hosting over 25,000 university doctoral dissertations, master's theses, and faculty working papers.
  • Plagiarism Detection Tools: Turnitin and Grammarly Premium accounts provided complimentary to all enrolled researchers.

Library Membership & Application Instructions

All registered undergraduate students, graduate scholars, and faculty members are automatically eligible for library privileges:

  1. Step 1: ID Card Activation: Present your university Smart ID card at the Circulation Desk on the 1st floor.
  2. Step 2: Digital Portal PIN: Set up your library OPAC password and remote proxy credentials at the circulation kiosk.
  3. Step 3: Barcode Verification: Verify your active enrollment status for borrowing privileges (up to 6 books simultaneously for undergraduates, 12 for graduates).
  4. Step 4: Orientation Tour: Attend a 15-minute library resource and database orientation held every Monday at 11:00 AM.

Library Code of Conduct & Rules

  • A valid university Smart ID card must be presented for library entrance and book circulation.
  • Mobile devices must be kept on silent mode; voice phone calls are strictly prohibited inside reading halls.
  • Beverages in spill-proof covered containers are permitted; hot food and snacks are confined to the ground floor cafe lounge.
  • Standard borrowing duration is 21 days for general books, renewable twice online if not reserved by another patron.
  • Overdue fines are charged at $0.50 per book per day to encourage timely circulation among students.
