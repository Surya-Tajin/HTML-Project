Graduate Studies Overview

The School of Graduate Studies at Metropolitan University fosters interdisciplinary scientific research, high-impact publication, and senior corporate management skills. Offering M.Sc., M.Eng., and MBA degrees, our programs connect students directly with industry advisory councils and funded research grants.

Featured Graduate Programs

  • Master of Science in Computer Science (M.Sc. CS): Advanced artificial intelligence, cryptography, natural language processing, and distributed systems.
  • Master of Science in Data Science (M.Sc. DS): Big data architecture, predictive analytics, statistical modeling, and deep learning algorithms.
  • Master of Science in Electrical Engineering (M.Sc. EE): Photonics, nanotechnology, autonomous mechatronics, and smart electrical grids.
  • Master of Business Administration (MBA): Executive corporate finance, strategic venture management, and global supply chain logistics.

Admission Requirements & Eligibility

Candidates applying for graduate and master's degree enrollment must meet the following standards:

  • A four-year bachelor's degree in a relevant discipline from an accredited institution.
  • Minimum undergraduate cumulative GPA of 3.00 out of 4.00 (or equivalent First Class honors).
  • Competitive GRE General Test scores (recommended minimum 310 quantitative/verbal for M.Sc. programs).
  • For MBA applicants: Minimum 2 years of verifiable professional corporate or managerial experience.
  • English language proficiency: TOEFL iBT score of 90 or IELTS Academic band score of 7.0 minimum.

Assistantships & Fellowships

  • Graduate Research Assistantship (GRA): Full 100% tuition coverage plus a monthly living stipend of $1,800 in exchange for 20 hours/week laboratory research.
  • Graduate Teaching Assistantship (GTA): Tuition remission and stipend for leading undergraduate laboratory and tutoring discussion sessions.
  • Industry Corporate Sponsorship: Co-funded graduate fellowships with tech titans for applied data science research.

Step-by-Step Application Guide

  1. Identify a Faculty Advisor: Review department faculty research papers and contact prospective thesis mentors whose laboratory interests align with yours.
  2. Submit Digital Dossier: Complete the online graduate application, submit curriculum vitae (CV), and upload official undergraduate transcripts.
  3. Statement of Research Objectives: Provide an 800-word essay articulating your proposed thesis topic, methodological approach, and long-term academic ambitions.
  4. Recommendation Letters: Solicit three confidential recommendation letters from former university professors or senior research managers.
  5. Interview & Acceptance: Departmental admissions committees interview prospective candidates prior to issuing funded admissions contracts.
