Bachelor of Science in Computer Science & Engineering

A comprehensive 4-year professional engineering program engineered to develop world-class software architects, AI researchers, and technological visionaries.

Home > Undergraduate Programs > Computer Science & Engineering

Program Overview

The Bachelor of Science in Computer Science and Engineering (B.Sc. in CSE) is an internationally accredited four-year academic curriculum designed to balance rigorous scientific foundations with hands-on software development, machine learning, and systems architecture.

Students work in advanced hardware robotics labs, cloud computing clusters, and cyber security testbeds. Graduates consistently secure roles at leading technology enterprises, top global research institutions, and groundbreaking tech startups.

Admission Requirements & Eligibility Criteria

Prospective applicants must satisfy the following academic prerequisites for undergraduate admission:

  • Minimum GPA of 3.50 (on a 5.00 scale) in Secondary (SSC / O-Level) examinations.
  • Minimum GPA of 3.50 in Higher Secondary (HSC / A-Level) with mandatory coursework in Mathematics and Physics.
  • Satisfactory score in the Metropolitan University Entrance Assessment or standardized SAT Reasoning Test.
  • Demonstrated English language proficiency (IELTS 6.5, TOEFL iBT 80, or university equivalent).

Required Documents

Applicants must assemble the following verified documents for submission:

  • Original Academic Transcripts and Certificates from secondary and higher secondary boards.
  • Government-issued National ID Card, Birth Certificate, or Valid International Passport.
  • Three recent passport-size color photographs (white background).
  • Two letters of academic recommendation from prior mathematics or science educators.
  • Statement of Purpose (500 words outlining intellectual motivation and career goals).

Application Procedure

  1. Step 1: Online Registration: Fill out the digital admissions application through the Metropolitan portal.
  2. Step 2: Document Upload: Upload scanned, notarized copies of all transcripts, identity credentials, and recommendation letters.
  3. Step 3: Application Fee Payment: Settle the non-refundable processing fee of $45 via bank transfer or online payment gateway.
  4. Step 4: Entrance Examination: Attend the university computer-based assessment covering Mathematics, Physics, and English Logic.
  5. Step 5: Viva & Admission Offer: Shortlisted candidates undergo a brief faculty interview followed by the official letter of acceptance.

Course Duration & Curriculum Structure

The program is structured over 4 academic years divided into 8 semesters, totaling 148 credit hours.

Semester Key Subject Modules Credits
Year 1 (Sem 1 & 2) Structured Programming (C/C++), Calculus, Physics, Discrete Mathematics, Digital Logic Design 36
Year 2 (Sem 3 & 4) Data Structures, Object-Oriented Programming (Java/Python), Algorithms, Computer Architecture, Database Systems 38
Year 3 (Sem 5 & 6) Operating Systems, Computer Networks, Software Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Microprocessors 38
Year 4 (Sem 7 & 8) Machine Learning, Distributed Cloud Systems, Cyber Security, Senior Capstone Project & Internship 36
