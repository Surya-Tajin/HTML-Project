Essential Gear Manifest

The 72-Hour Survival "Go-Bag"

A Grab-and-Go Bag (Go-Bag) is a lightweight, durable, water-resistant backpack packed with vital survival provisions to sustain an individual for at least three full days without external electricity or municipal running water.

Keep your family go-bag in an easily accessible location near your main exit door (such as a hallway coat hook or front closet). Every family member should know its exact location.

Six-Month Rotation Rule

Every six months (at the change of monsoon seasons), inspect your kit: replace expiring medications and food, refresh bottled water, and test battery levels in radios and flashlights.

1. Water & Hydration

✓
4 litres bottled water per person per day (3 days = 12 litres per person).
✓
100 Water purification tablets (Halazone or Sodium Dichloroisocyanurate).
✓
Oral Rehydration Salts (ORS): 10 packets to counter dehydration from diarrheal outbreaks.

2. Food & Cooking Supplies

✓
Ready-to-eat dry foods: Flattened rice (Chira), jaggery (Gur), high-energy glucose biscuits, nuts.
✓
Canned proteins: Canned beans, fish, or ready meals with pull-tabs or manual can opener.
✓
Stainless steel cup & spoon per family member.

3. Medical & First Aid

✓
7-Day supply of chronic medications (Insulin, blood pressure pills, asthma inhalers).
✓
First aid kit: Sterile gauze pads, roller bandages, adhesive tape, antiseptic solution, safety shears.
✓
Burn dressing & paracetamol tablets for pain and fever reduction.

4. Power & Communication

✓
Battery-operated AM/FM radio with 3 complete spare battery sets.
✓
High-lumen LED flashlight & headlamp with spare batteries.
✓
Heavy-duty 20,000 mAh power bank kept 100% charged with appropriate charging cables.

5. Emergency Tools & Signaling

✓
Pea-less safety whistle: Audible over 1 km away to guide search-and-rescue teams.
✓
Multi-tool or swiss army pocket knife: With can opener, pliers, and knife blade.
✓
Waterproof matchbox / flint fire starter and candle stubs in a sealed plastic bag.

6. Waterproof Documents & Cash

✓
Waterproof document dry-bag: National ID (NID), passports, property deeds, vaccination cards.
✓
Emergency cash in small banknotes: ATMs and electronic POS terminals will be offline without power.
✓
Laminated emergency contact sheet: With phone numbers of relatives and doctors written in waterproof ink.
Household Safety Architecture

The 4-Step Family Emergency Plan

Disasters strike when families are separated at school, work, or transit. Establish this reunification protocol today.

📍 1. Local Meeting Point

Designate an exact landmark outside your home (e.g., the neighborhood school gate or big banyan tree) if an evacuation occurs while you are near home.

🏢 2. Regional Meeting Point

Designate an elevated public building outside your immediate neighborhood (e.g., District Upazila Complex or Upazila Cyclone Shelter) if the entire village is inundated.

📞 3. Out-of-District Contact

Designate an out-of-district relative (e.g., an aunt in Sylhet or Rajshahi). During disasters, long-distance SMS text messages often go through when local circuits are jammed.

🏫 4. School & Workplace Policy

Obtain the disaster release procedures for children's schools. Verify who has formal authorization to pick up your children if parents are stranded by floodwaters.

Civil Evacuation Infrastructure

Public Cyclone & Flood Shelter Protocol

Multi-Purpose Cyclone Shelters (MPCS) and Flood Shelters are reinforced elevated concrete structures engineered to withstand 200+ km/h gale forces and 6-metre tidal surge crests.

Knowing what to expect and how to conduct yourself inside a community shelter ensures order, dignity, and hygiene during high-stress evacuations:

  • Arrival Registration: Check in immediately with the Camp Committee or Red Crescent volunteer desk. State all family members present and note any separated relatives.
  • Space Allocation: Respect designated zones for women, nursing mothers, and unaccompanied children. Keep passageways and stairwells completely clear of bags.
  • Sanitation Hygiene: Boil or chemically chlorinate all drinking water from shelter tube-wells. Use communal latrines responsibly to avoid cholera outbreaks.
  • Livestock Stabling: Domestic cattle and goats must be stationed at the ground-level open plinth or adjoining earthen killa mound—never inside human residential sleeping halls.

What to Bring to the Shelter

✓
Your 72-Hour Survival Go-Bag with dry food, water, and prescription medicines.
✓
Mylar thermal blankets & woven plastic sleeping mats for concrete floors.
✓
Waterproof flashlight & personal hygiene pouch (soap, sanitary pads).
✓
Baby food, clean feeding bottles, & clean cotton wraps for infants.
