24/7 First Response Protocol

Are You Experiencing an Active Emergency Right Now?

Stay calm. Your safety is paramount. Follow our 3-step immediate safety rule before attempting any intervention:

  1. Assess Danger: Ensure the scene is safe for you. Do not become a second casualty.
  2. Call Dispatch: Dial 999 immediately for professional ambulance, fire, or police dispatch. State your exact location first.
  3. Basic First Care Only: Apply direct pressure on severe bleeding or cool fresh burns with clean cool water. Never perform uncertified invasive maneuvers.

Family & Household Resilience

The Master 72-Hour Survival Checklist

During major floods, earthquakes, or storm surges, municipal rescue services may take up to 72 hours to reach isolated sectors. Assemble this kit today.

Water & Essential Nutrition

✓
4 Litres of water per person per day (3-day minimum supply for drinking & sanitation).
✓
Water purification tablets (chlorine/halazone) & oral rehydration salts (ORS packets).
✓
Non-perishable ready-to-eat food (flattened rice, biscuits, canned proteins, nuts).
✓
Manual can opener & disposable utensils.

Medical & Personal Care

✓
Sterile gauze pads, bandages, & medical tape for wound management.
✓
7-day reserve of prescription medications (insulin, heart/blood pressure pills).
✓
Antiseptic solution, burn dressing, and safety shears.
✓
Sanitary hygiene supplies & wet wipes for waterless decontamination.

Power, Signal & Records

✓
Battery-powered AM/FM radio with 3 complete spare battery sets.
✓
High-lumen LED flashlight & loud safety whistle for acoustic distress signalling.
✓
Waterproof pouch for critical documents (NID, birth certificates, deeds, cash).
✓
Heavy-duty power bank & waterproof matchbox.
